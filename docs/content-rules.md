
# FARMCLUB OS Content Rules

## Voice

The FARMCLUB OS voice should be:

- Confident
- Clear
- Forward-looking
- Credible
- Inclusive
- Practical
- Institutionally professional

## Writing Style

- Prefer concise sentences.
- Avoid excessive buzzwords.
- Avoid long paragraphs.
- Use active language.
- Do not repeat the same proposition in multiple sections.
- Do not invent evidence, partnerships or statistics.

## Terminology

Use:

- FARMCLUB OS
- Experience and Innovation Hubs
- Agricultural entrepreneurs
- Agri-enterprises
- Platform ecosystem
- Knowledge Centre
- Enterprise development
- Market access
- Impact intelligence

Do not use:

- FARMCLUS OS
- Farm Club OS
- Ikorodu Hub
- Ibadan Hub
- Proposed partner language presented as confirmed
